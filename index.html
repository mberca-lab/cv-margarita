PsychometricsReliabilityRelative weight analysis

Measurement study

Methods & interpretation
Analytical approach
Examined how survey items grouped and their internal consistency before analysing readiness associations through regression, correlation and relative weight analysis.
Interpretation
Structure and reliability tests address aspects of measurement quality, not all forms of validity. Associated-factor analyses do not establish causal effects.

05 / How I work

Start with the question. Stay with the evidence.

?

Make the question answerable.

Clarify the decision, test the measurement and choose an analytical approach that fits the problem.

↗

Make the answer useful.

Explain the findings, their limitations and the practical options—not just the statistical result.

↻

Make the work reusable.

Build documented methods, reusable code and clear handovers so recurring work can be rerun rather than rebuilt.
